# Task 022 — Desktop Workflow Management & Profiles

## Role

Act as a Principal Rust Engineer, Senior Tauri 2 Engineer, React/TypeScript Architect, Workflow Orchestration Engineer, and macOS Desktop UX Engineer.

You are implementing **Task 022 — Desktop Workflow Management & Profiles** of the Service Orchestrator project.

Your objective is to deliver a complete, production-quality desktop interface for planning, executing, monitoring, and managing development environment workflows.

This is a single end-to-end implementation task.

Do not stop after planning or scaffolding. Implement, test, fix, document, and report the completed work.
